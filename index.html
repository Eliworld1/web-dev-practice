About God of War

God of War is an action-adventure video game series. The games follow Kratos, a warrior who faces powerful gods and creatures.

The 2018 God of War game takes place in the world of Norse mythology. Kratos travels with his son Atreus as they begin a journey together.

Why I Like God of War

I like God of War because of its story, characters, and gameplay. The game also has exciting battles and a large world to explore.
